# Hitch Pass — Growth Playbook

Zero-budget organic growth plan for reaching Thousand Trails / Encore / ELS members.
Built from two fact-checked research passes (July 3, 2026). Findings are labeled
**Verified** (survived adversarial verification) vs **Lead** (search-surfaced, real URL,
not double-checked because the fact-checker hit rate limits). Act on Verified with
confidence; treat Leads as "go look yourself before you post."

All pricing is 2025–2026 and changes annually. Re-check before quoting externally.

---

## 1. Positioning (Verified)

The durable claim is the gap, not the price. Every major RV app is a trip planner or a
campground finder. **None of them holds your third-party club memberships.** Togo RV /
Roadtrippers, Campendium, and RV LIFE were all checked: they name Thousand Trails only as a
discount partner, never as something you track. That empty space is the entire product.

Lead line: **"Every app plans your trip. None of them hold your membership. Hitch Pass does."**

Price is a secondary proof point, not the headline. RV LIFE Pro is $65/yr and Campendium RV
Pro is $59.99/yr, so $54/yr undercuts both, but RV LIFE routinely discounts the first year to
about $48.75 via coupons, so "cheapest" is fragile. "The only one that holds your memberships"
is not.

### Not-affiliated disclaimer (paste verbatim)

> Hitch Pass is an independent app and is not authorized, sponsored, or otherwise approved by
> Thousand Trails, Encore, or Equity LifeStyle Properties, Inc. All trademarks are the property
> of their respective owners.

Naming rule (nominative use): reference the brands only as "for use with" or "compatible with,"
and never put "Thousand Trails" inside a product name. "Hitch Pass" is already clean.

## 3. Product hooks members actually struggle with (Verified vs official rules)

Both confirmed against the official Thousand Trails member-rules PDF. These are the explainer
topics that rank in search and the features that earn a soft mention in a forum thread.

1. **Max-stay rules.** Base Camping Pass: 14 consecutive days, then 7 nights out. Enhanced
   tiers (Journey/Explore/Adventure): 21 days, park-to-park. Confusing enough that members ask
   about it constantly. Hitch Pass already models this per-tier.
2. **Trails Collection.** The paid add-on ($214/yr, verified against the official TT page —
   the earlier ~$450–475 figure was wrong) that decides which Encore parks (the ones showing the
   TT icon) you can actually book. 60-day booking window, up to 14 nights, ~$20/night at fee
   resorts. Members find the park map misleading. Natural explainer content and wallet feature.

## 4. Channel playbook

### Verified channels (act with confidence)

- **Facebook — "Thousand Trails (Outdoor World) & Encore — The Good AND The Bad"**
  (facebook.com/groups/594453579020342). The one TT/Encore-specific group whose existence held
  up. Candid experience-sharing group. *Enter:* join, answer booking-window and park-quality
  questions helpfully first; only a member-to-member "here's a free wallet I use" framing fits,
  never a promo post.
- **Reddit — r/RVLiving (~219k).** Largest confirmed audience, general RV not TT-specific.
  *Enter:* the 90/10 rule (roughly 90% genuine help, under 10% self-mention) and "be a redditor
  with a website, not a website with a Reddit account." Build comment history first, disclose you
  built it, never cross-post the same link across subs.
- **Blog — lovethatrv.com/work-with-us.** Accepts guest-post submissions for editorial review,
  with contributor bios that link back. *Enter:* pitch an article idea (e.g. the 14-day rule or
  Trails Collection explainer), link the tool in your bio.
- **iRV2 — not the minefield it was assumed to be.** The "iRV2 bans non-advertiser promotion"
  claim was refuted in verification. Its exact vendor policy is still unconfirmed, so read the
  rules, but do not write it off.

(rules-compliant by design) and soft presence in the two "Good and Bad" Facebook groups.
