The 21-day rule shouldn't require a spreadsheet

Tracking stay limits across multiple RV membership networks gets confusing fast, especially for full-timers juggling Thousand Trails, Passport America, and a handful of others. Every network counts its nights a little differently, and one miscount can cost you your next booking window.

So most people fall back on a spreadsheet, a notes app, or a best guess. That works until it doesn't, and by then you have already overstayed or missed the day your next window opened.

Hitch Pass replaces all of that. Log a stay and it does the counting for you, keeps every membership in one wallet, and shows your parks on a single map. No spreadsheet, no guessing.
