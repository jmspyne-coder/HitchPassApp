6. THE GATE SPLIT — WHAT JIM IS ACTUALLY DECIDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FAILING FINDING IS CORRECT AND IS NOT FULLY FIXABLE.
syncAccountWallet() reads legacyClaimedBy(), then writes the claim. Those are two separate
localStorage operations, and NO BROWSER OFFERS COMPARE-AND-SET FOR localStorage. Two tabs
signing in two DIFFERENT keyless accounts at genuinely the same instant can both read an
unclaimed slot and both adopt the legacy wallet. A write-then-read-back narrows the window to
two adjacent synchronous statements; it does not close it. No implementation closes it.

WHY IT IS NOT THE SAME CLASS AS THE TWO EARLIER FINDINGS:
  - Worst outcome is DISCLOSURE — an account seeing the device's existing cards.
  - main does exactly that TODAY, unconditionally, for every account (the T-2 shared blob).
  - Nothing is destroyed. The two destruction defects ARE closed, and both reviewing gates
    reproduced them as closed rather than taking the builder's word for it.
  - Reaching it needs: two different accounts, both keyless, both first-signing-in, same device,
    two tabs, within microseconds, during the one-time migration window.

RECOMMENDATION: merge. The residual is strictly no worse than current production behavior, and
it blocks a change that closes two confirmed unrecoverable-data-loss defects and completes FL-H3.
